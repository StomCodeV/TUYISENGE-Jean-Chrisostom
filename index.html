About Me

Dedicated student teacher & developer shaping the future of education in Rwanda.

Student Teacher & EdTech Builder

I'm a final-year student teacher in Language Education at TTC Muhanga, and a self-taught developer who believes every future teacher deserves digital skills — not fear of the unknown.

That belief became iMENA Hub, an EdTech initiative that has now trained 54+ student teachers and youth across Rwanda. I build tools — from this 3D portfolio to the Certificate Verification System and Paperless assessment platform.

My approach: disciplined work ethic, creative problem-solving, and a commitment to lifelong learning. I create digital solutions that are functional, beautiful, and human-centered.

My Journey

From classroom to ecosystem — every step closing the gap between education and technology.

Founded · 2025

Building · 2026

Vision · Next
